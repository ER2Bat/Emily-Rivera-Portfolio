About Me and Contact Info

Emily Rivera is a UI Designer from the Bay Area currently earning a BFA in Art with concentration
in Interaction & Game Design from California State University, East Bay in May 2027. With an interest in how an app or website operates, she is skilled in designing buttons,
navigation bars, sliders, icons, and containers. She takes inspiration from video game menus, and heads-up displays when designing an app or website.
With the use of the program called Figma, she plans out her ideas and creates wireframes which are the blueprint of how an app or website will be formatted.
She thinks of how users will interact with a specific part of the app or website to create a better user experience.
